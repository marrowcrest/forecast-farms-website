Horses we train

Every horse starts where it is.

We train horses and ponies at every stage, with the same priorities: correct basics, confidence, and a horse that is willing to do its job.

Young and green horses

Correct basics from the start: rhythm, straightness, steering, and a calm introduction to fences and new places.

Off-track Thoroughbreds

Patient restarting for horses coming off the track, building toward a second career in the hunter ring.

Experienced horses

Tune-ups for horses that have lost rhythm or confidence, or that need sharpening before the season.

Show prospects and ponies

Preparation for the hunter, jumper and equitation rings, with show experience added as the horse is ready.

Our approach

Honesty and patience, and a horse that wants to work.

Training is built on honesty, integrity and patience. The goal is a confident horse that understands its job and does it willingly.

  • Schooling at home before showsSkills are confirmed in our ring before they are tested away from home
  • Flatwork underneath everythingRhythm, straightness and adjustability come before height
  • Confidence before pressureEach new question is introduced when the horse is ready for it
  • One trainer for horse and riderThe person schooling your horse also coaches you

How it works

From evaluation to a better ride.

Every horse starts with an evaluation, and every plan is agreed with the owner before training begins.

  1. 1

    Evaluate

    Denise rides or watches your horse to assess its training and way of going, and hears your goals for the horse.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Together we agree on goals, where the horse will be based, and how often it is ridden, then set a plan for the season.

  3. 3

    Train

    Training rides on the flat and over fences, with show outings added once the horse is ready for them.

  4. 4

    Report and ride

    You hear how the horse is progressing, and lessons on your own horse let you feel the changes for yourself.

Does my horse need to stay at the farm?

Where your horse is based is part of the training plan. Tell us your situation when you request an evaluation, and we will talk through the options.

How often will my horse be ridden?

That depends on the horse’s age, fitness and goals. We set the schedule with you when we agree on the plan.

Will I still ride my horse?

Yes, and we recommend it. Lessons on your own horse turn the training into a partnership you can ride.

Do you train ponies?

Yes. We train horses and ponies, including ponies being prepared for junior riders.

Can you get my horse ready to sell?

Yes. Horses in our consignment program are kept in work and presented to suitable buyers. See Horse Search & Sales.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the training plan. We confirm it after your horse’s evaluation.
