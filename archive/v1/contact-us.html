Evaluation lesson

Start with an evaluation lesson.

Every new rider and horse at Forecast Farms begins here. In one lesson, Denise sees where you are, talks through your goals, and recommends the training that fits you and your horse.

How it works

From request to plan, in three steps.

The evaluation is a working lesson. You ride, Denise watches closely, and you hear plainly what she sees.

  1. 1

    Before

    Send your request

    Tell us about the rider, the horse and your goals. We follow up to schedule a time, answer your questions and, if you are bringing your own horse, arrange the ship-in.

  2. 2

    During

    Ride with Denise

    Denise assesses position, balance and effectiveness on the flat and, when it suits the rider, over fences. With your own horse, she also looks at the horse’s training and way of going.

  3. 3

    After

    Leave with a plan

    You hear what is working and what needs attention, then get a recommended pathway, a lesson and training schedule, and first goals for the season.

Preparing for your lesson

What we look at, and what to bring.

Knowing what Denise is watching for helps you get the most from the lesson.

The rider

  • Position and security in the saddle
  • Balance and rhythm at the walk, trot and canter
  • Clear, consistent use of the aids
  • Jumping position and eye for a distance, if jumping
  • Riding history, goals and show experience

Your horse

If you bring your own

  • Rhythm, straightness and way of going
  • Response to the aids and adjustability
  • Jumping form and attitude over fences
  • Fitness, soundness and tack fit
  • Training history and show record

What to bring

  • A properly fitted, ASTM/SEI-certified riding helmet
  • Boots with a heel, and breeches or riding tights
  • Gloves, if you usually ride in them
  • For a ship-in: a current negative Coggins and vaccination records
  • Your horse’s tack, fitted and in good repair

Questions

Before you book

Do I need my own horse?

No. Riders train on their own horse, ship in for lessons, or ride one of our experienced schoolmasters. Tell us in your request and we will plan the lesson around it.

What level should I be?

We train junior and adult riders who want to ride consistently and progress, from riders building a solid foundation to riders preparing for the show ring. The evaluation shows us where to start. Riders ages 4 to 7 begin in our Junior Riding Club.

I want training for my horse. How does that start?

The same way. Choose Horse Training in the form and tell us about your horse’s age, experience and goals. We will arrange the best way to assess the horse.

How much does the evaluation cost?

We confirm the current rate when we schedule your lesson. Call or email us if you would like it before you send a request.

What happens after the evaluation?

We recommend a pathway, a schedule and first goals, and you decide whether to go ahead. Lessons and training begin once the farm’s agreements and release forms are signed.

Do you offer trail rides or one-time rides?

No. Our program is built for riders and horses who train consistently.
