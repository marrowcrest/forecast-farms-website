04Intrinsic risks of equine activities

Horseback riding and other equine activities carry intrinsic dangers, including:

  • A horse’s tendency to behave in ways that may injure people around it
  • Unpredictable reactions to noises, sudden movements, and unfamiliar objects, people or animals
  • Ground surface and subsurface conditions
  • Collisions with other animals or objects
  • A rider’s own failure to keep control or to ride within their ability

Virginia’s equine activity liability law (Code of Virginia §§ 3.2-6200 through 3.2-6203) limits the liability of equine professionals and activity sponsors for injuries caused by these intrinsic dangers. Nothing on this website changes any release or assumption of risk you sign with us.
